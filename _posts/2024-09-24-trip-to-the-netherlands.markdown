I had not traveled that much that year until then.
At the beginning of September, I saw that Epica - my favorite band - was still
selling tickets for their The Symphonic Synergy show in Amsterdam.
So I spontaneously decided to take the FlixBus to the Netherlands.

[![A slightly slanted building in Amsterdam][architecture_01]][architecture_01]{:.image-left}
[![A house with beautiful window shutters][architecture_02]][architecture_02]{:.image-right}

I arrived in Amsterdam Sloterdijk on Thursday morning and went for a lengthy
walk through the city, over all the canals and past the beautiful buildings
that were built centuries ago.

[![A flowerpot on a street lamp][flowers]][flowers]{:.float-left}
One of the first things I noticed in Amsterdam was the flowers.
They were everywhere along the Brouwersgracht and later the Prinsengracht.
There are several flower boxes on every bridge, on every window and also on some
street lamps and traffic signs.
This gave the city a nice touch.

[![Epica's show][epica_01]][epica_01]{:.image-left}
[![Epica on stage with their logo prominently shown in the background][epica_02]][epica_02]{:.image-right}

In the evening I then saw Epica's show at the AFAS Live.
It was fantastic!
The whole show lasted about 2 hours and 15 minutes and the band, orchestra, and
all choirs gave a great performance.

[![View on a street in Utrecht][utrecht_01]][utrecht_01]{:.image-left}
[![A channel in Utrecht][utrecht_02]][utrecht_02]{:.image-right}

On Friday morning, I first took an Intercity train to Utrecht.
Utrecht is one of the oldest cities in the Netherlands with a long and important
history.
There are many beautiful streets with old buildings.
Apart from a few places in the center, the city was not as crowded with tourists
as Amsterdam.

[![The Erasmusbrug in Rotterdam][erasmusbrug]][erasmusbrug]

In the second half of Friday, I continued my journey on the Intercity to
Rotterdam.
There I first explored some of the usual sights, such as the Erasmusbrug or ...

[![The inside of the Markthal in Rotterdam][rotterdam_market]][rotterdam_market]{:.image-left}
[![The habour of Rotterdam][rotterdam_harbor]][rotterdam_harbor]{:.image-right}

... the beautiful Markthal and the harbor.
Even with the view from the Euromast, I couldn't quite grasp how large the harbor
actually is.

[![Skaters in Rotterdam][rotterdam_skating]][rotterdam_skating]{:.image-left}
[![A street festival in Rotterdam with a few people dancing to music][street_festival]][street_festival]{:.image-right}

On my evening walk through Rotterdam, the city seemed so vibrant.
People were having fun on the streets everywhere.
Finally, on the way to a bar, I stumbled across a small street party and decided
to stay there.

[![A windmill with the flag of the Netherlands in front of it][windmill_01]][windmill_01]{:.image-left}
[![Two windmills][windmill_02]][windmill_02]{:.image-right}

[![Multiple windmills on the horizon][zaanse_schans_01]][zaanse_schans_01]{:.image-left}
[![Several old houses on the shore][zaanse_schans_02]][zaanse_schans_02]{:.image-right}

When visiting the Netherlands, seeing the windmills was, of course, a must.
So on my last day, I traveled to Zaanse Schans.
Even on the train ride, it was clear that this was a tourist hotspot, as it was
the only station that was announced in English.
Nevertheless, despite the many tourists, you could walk around the place
relatively undisturbed.

[![A police van in front of multiple tents][police_01]][police_01]{:.image-left}
[![A trailer from the Technisches Hilfswerk with a lightmast in front of a FlixBus][police_02]][police_02]{:.image-right}

On Saturday evening, I took a night bus back to Germany.
Unfortunately, we were held up for about 45 to 60 minutes at a police
checkpoint near the border.
Thanks for that.
Traveling the European Union without restrictions was nice while it lasted.
