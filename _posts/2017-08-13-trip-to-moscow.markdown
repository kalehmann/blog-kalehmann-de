In the fall of 2016, my cousin first suggested that we visit Moscow together.
It started as a joke, but by the end of the year, the plans had become more
concrete.
Ultimately, it became a seven-day trip in August 2017.

Our journey began in Prague, where we flew directly to Moscow Sheremetyevo
International Airport.
The outbound flight was a bit strange.
Since the plane was overbooked, we almost had to sit in jump seats in the
kitchen.
Eventually, a few other passengers didn't show up in time, so we ended up with
the boring regular seats after all.

The city was a completely different experience to anything I had seen before.
There were really impressive and well-maintained monuments everywhere, as well
as decayed residential blocks all over the city.
One of the first places we came across was the Red Square with the Kremlin.

![A view of the Kremlin]({{ "assets/17-moscow-trip/kreml.jpg" | absolute_url }})

Of course, we also visited the Lenin Mausoleum there.
After waiting for half an hour in the line to the entrance, we finally got to
take the few-minute tour through the mausoleum.
Oddly enough, there was a guard checking our bags at the entrance and he took
a bottle of Sternburg beer, which I brought from Germany, out of my backpack and
examined it thoroughly - I was absolutely convinced he wouldn't let me bring it
inside - but then he just looked at me, nodded, and put it back into my backpack.


What impressed me most while touring through the city were all the beautiful
and magnificent parks scattered throughout it.

The main entrance to the Gorky Central Park of Culture and Leisure, named after
the Soviet writer Maxim Gorky:
![The beautiful main portal of the Gorky Park]({{ "assets/17-moscow-trip/moscow_gorky_park_main_portal.jpg" | absolute_url }})

A monument to honor the fallen soldiers of the First World War in front of
Victory Park
![A monument at Victory Park in Moscow]({{ "assets/17-moscow-trip/monument_victory_park_moscow.jpg" | absolute_url }})

Welcome to Losiny Ostrov National Park, also called Elk Island.
This is the view outside Belokamennaya station in the park.
We didn't see any elk, but we did come across several of their tracks.
![Outside the Belokamennaya station in the Elk Island National Park, some abandoned building with graffiti]({{ "assets/17-moscow-trip/moscow_elk_island_national_park_belokamennaya_station.jpg" | absolute_url }})

Kolomenskoye, a former royal estate in Moscow.
![Kolomenskoye park]({{ "assets/17-moscow-trip/moscow_kolomenskoye.jpg" | absolute_url }})


Another attraction we visited was the Ostankino Tower.
This was the view over the city from the observation platform at about 350
metres above the ground.
